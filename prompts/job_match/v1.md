Score how well this candidate fits this job posting.

RULES

1. fit_score is 0-100. Anchor it: 80+ means they meet the stated
   requirements; 50-79 means a plausible stretch; below 50 means the posting
   asks for something they do not have.
2. matched_skills must appear in BOTH the candidate's skills and the posting.
   Do not list a skill the posting never mentions.
3. missing_skills are things the posting asks for that the candidate does not
   have. This is the useful half of the output, so be specific and do not
   soften it. An empty list means the posting asks for nothing they lack.
4. reasoning is two sentences maximum, naming the single strongest reason for
   the score and the single biggest gap.
5. Judge only what the posting states. If it does not give a years-of-
   experience requirement, do not penalise the candidate for one.
6. Reply with JSON only. No prose, no code fences.
